## Why StrideZero?

Most fitness trackers harvest your data. StrideZero is built differently. It is a strictly **private-first** application designed with a lightweight footprint, avoiding large native map SDKs, bundled world maps, and heavy analytics. It features "Stride Blue" deep navy surfaces with highly customizable palettes (Ocean, Velocity, Violet, Sunset) and a focused exercise mode that hides navigation while you move.

## Key Features

### Privacy & Data Ownership
*   **100% Local Data:** Local history, route points, activity metrics, and GPX 1.1 exports.
*   **Encrypted Backups:** PIN-encrypted `.szbackup` transfer of your complete history and settings.
*   **Storage Independence:** Save backups to Google Drive, OneDrive, Dropbox, or local storage without needing a StrideZero server.

### Smart Navigation & Tracking
*   **Hardware Optimized:** Leverages your phone's built-in step counter with an accelerometer/gyroscope fallback.
*   **Intelligent Location:** GPS-first location with cell/Wi-Fi fallback and a strict movement-evidence gate to prevent stationary GPS wander.
*   **Live Workouts:** Active sessions open a live navigation map with breadcrumb tracking, phone-heading camera options, and compact primary stats.
*   **Lightweight Maps:** Fast, bundled map renderer utilizing OpenStreetMap and Esri satellite imagery.

### Stride AI & Media Integration
*   **Stride AI:** Deterministic, private rules-and-metrics coaching on the phone (No login or internet required).
*   **Gemini Mode (Optional):** Advanced AI analysis using your own user-supplied key safely secured on your device.
*   **Smart Media:** Built-in Android media controls with launch buttons for YouTube and YT Music.
*   **Voice Cues:** Local Android text-to-speech cues for directions, optionally pausing when stationary.

## Honest Limits

Transparency is a core feature of this project:
*   **No Cloud Backend:** There is no developer-operated cloud account. Automatic syncing across devices is not supported to maintain absolute privacy.
*   **Media Controls:** Requires you to explicitly grant Android Notification access to control your active audio session. 
*   **Offline Maps:** Saved route geometry is available offline, but loading fresh street maps and satellite imagery requires an internet connection.
